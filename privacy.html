Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version
  • Your invoices, clients and other business data stay on your devices and, encrypted, in your own Google Drive. Keralogik never receives them.
  • MyBills only asks Google for its own hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see your other files.
  • If you subscribe to MyBills Premium, our billing server keeps only your Google account ID and email and your subscription status. Razorpay handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details.

1. Who we are and our role

MyBills (the “App”) is an invoicing application for Android and Windows developed by Keralogik (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy is our notice under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (together, the “DPDP Act”). It explains what personal data is used, why, for how long, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to the App, to our billing server (api.mybills.co.in) and to this website, mybills.co.in.

For your Google account information and your subscription records, Keralogik is the Data Fiduciary. The business information you enter into the App, including details of your own clients, is processed only on your devices and in your own Google Drive; Keralogik never receives it. Where that information includes personal data of your clients, you decide how it is used and you are responsible for it as their Data Fiduciary (see our Terms of Service).

No location, contacts, call logs, messages or browsing history. No advertising IDs, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services that send data to us. This website uses no cookies or analytics; it loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information (such as your IP address) from your browser.

3. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent. Before you use the App, it shows the list above and asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older and to agree, each with its own checkbox. Subscribing asks separately for your consent to share your email address with our billing server and Razorpay. We record when you consented and to which version of this notice.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: in Settings → Privacy & your data → Withdraw consent and delete everything, or by writing to us. MyBills then deletes your billing record, the copy in your Google Drive and the data on that device, signs out and removes its access to your Google account. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it. Without consent the App cannot work, because it needs your Google account to store your data.

We may also process personal data without consent where the DPDP Act allows it, for example to comply with a law or a court order.

5. Subscriptions and payments

MyBills Premium is sold in the App through Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited). When you subscribe, our billing server creates your subscription with Razorpay using your email address, and you enter payment details directly on Razorpay’s checkout. Razorpay processes them under its own Privacy Policy as a separate Data Fiduciary. We receive confirmation of payments and your subscription status, never your full card or bank details. The App keeps a copy of your subscription status on your device so that Premium works offline.

6. Sharing, processors and transfers

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. It is shared only:

  • with Google, to sign you in and to store your encrypted backup in your own Google Drive (Google’s Privacy Policy);
  • if you subscribe, with Razorpay for payments, and with the cloud provider that hosts our billing server (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure), which processes that data only on our behalf as our Data Processor, under security obligations;
  • when you choose to share, email, print or export a document; the receiving app or person handles that copy; and
  • where the law requires it, for example to tax authorities or under a court order.

Google and our hosting provider may store data on servers outside India. The DPDP Act permits such transfers except to countries the Government of India restricts; we will follow any such restriction.

7. Security

  • On your devices, your data is kept in the App’s private storage, and sign-in tokens in the operating system’s secure storage (Android Keystore / Windows protected storage).
  • In your Google Drive, every file is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. The key is created on your device and stored in your Drive app folder and in secure storage, so your devices can restore your data; the safety of your backup therefore depends on your Google account, so please turn on Google 2-Step Verification.
  • On our billing server, records are kept in an access-controlled database, connections use HTTPS (TLS), requests are verified with Google and rate-limited, and Razorpay notifications are checked with a signature. Access logs are kept for one year to detect and investigate misuse, then deleted.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep personal data only as long as it is needed for the purpose above, then delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it longer:

  • Data on your devices and in your Drive stays until you delete it. In the App: Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete your Google Drive copy (or, on the web, Google Drive Settings → Manage apps → MyBills → Delete hidden app data); to remove it from a device, sign out and choose Remove from device, or uninstall.
  • Subscription records are deleted automatically one year after a subscription ends, and unpaid checkouts after 30 days. You can delete yours sooner in Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete your billing data (cancel an active subscription first). Razorpay keeps its own payment records as the law requires.
  • Access logs are kept for one year.
  • Revoke Google access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we do not hold copies of your business data, we cannot recover it once you delete it. Tax laws may require you to keep invoices for several years; deleting them is your decision.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: a summary of your personal data and how it is processed. Settings → Privacy & your data shows what the App holds and, if you subscribe, everything our billing server holds; you can save a copy of both.
  • Correction, completion and updating: edit your business and client details in the App at any time. These stay editable even after a free trial ends.
  • Erasure: delete your Drive copy, your billing record or everything, as described above.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (section 3).
  • Grievance redressal: write to our contact below. We respond to every request and grievance as soon as possible and in any case within 90 days.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to: use Settings → Privacy & your data → Nominate someone, or write to us with your nominee’s details.

To protect you, we may ask you to write from, or sign in with, the Google account your request concerns. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you use MyBills to keep personal data about your own clients, they may make these requests to you; the App lets you correct or delete their details at any time.

10. Personal data breaches

If a breach affects personal data we hold, we will tell the affected users without delay, in plain language: what happened and when, the likely impact, what we are doing about it, what you can do to protect yourself, and whom to contact. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require, including a detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

11. Children

MyBills is a business tool for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it; the App asks you to confirm this. We do not knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18. If we learn that we hold such data, we will delete it.

12. Changes and language

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page with a new “Last updated” date, show you the new notice in the App and ask for your consent again before it applies to you. This notice is in English; if you would like it in another language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India, write to us.
