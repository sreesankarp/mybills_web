Private by design

Your invoices live in your Google Drive. Not on our servers.

MyBills has no servers that store your business data. You sign in with your Google account, and MyBills keeps an encrypted copy of your data in a private, hidden app folder inside your own Google Drive — so it syncs between your devices and comes back automatically if you change phones.

Encrypted with AES-256

Every file is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded to your Drive.

Only its own folder

MyBills asks Google only for access to its own app data folder. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data. Your clients' details are yours alone.

Questions

Good to know

Why does MyBills need my Google account?

Signing in with Google lets MyBills store an encrypted backup of your data in your own Google Drive and keep your phone and PC in sync. MyBills only requests access to its own hidden app data folder — it cannot read your other Drive files, emails or contacts.

Can the MyBills team see my invoices?

No. Your data is stored on your devices and in your own Google Drive, encrypted before upload. We do not operate servers that receive or store your business data.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can create, edit and issue invoices offline. Changes upload automatically when you are back online.

What if I create invoices on two devices at the same time?

Each device numbers in its own series (for example INV/25-26/A/0001 and INV/25-26/B/0001), so numbers can never clash — even offline. GST rules allow multiple invoice series.

Is MyBills a replacement for my accountant?

MyBills calculates taxes and prepares GST reports from the invoices you create, but you remain responsible for the accuracy of your invoices and returns. We recommend reviewing filings with your tax professional.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Data & backup → Delete cloud data, then sign out and choose to remove data from the device. You can also revoke MyBills' access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will MyBills stay free?

MyBills is free during early access. We plan to introduce an optional paid plan with advanced features later; we'll announce pricing clearly in advance and the core invoicing you rely on will keep working.
